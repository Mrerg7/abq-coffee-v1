**ABQ.Coffee** reads as a complete brand before a logo exists: city, category, and purpose-built extension in one hostname. That is rare, and it is the whole argument.

## The two halves each do a job

- **ABQ** — the city, as everyone actually writes it. Not "Albuquerque" spelled out on a window decal or a cup sleeve — the three-letter shorthand residents use on signs, hashtags, and maps pins. Locals type it. Visitors learn it in a day.
- **.Coffee** — the category, in the extension itself. Not `.com` with a subtitle, not a made-up word that needs explaining. The TLD says the business before the headline does.

Put together: *Albuquerque coffee*. Say it once on the phone and it types itself.

## Why the exact-match domain matters

When the name and the domain are the same string, every channel reinforces the others:

- The **window decal** and the **search result** are identical.
- The **Google Business Profile** name, the **domain**, and the **meta title** all match — which is exactly what local search rewards.
- Word-of-mouth referrals arrive as a typed query that resolves on the first try.

Split those — ABQRoasters.com with a subtitle, ABQCafe.net, Albuquerque-Coffee-House.com — and every mention loses a little. The exact-match form is the one that compounds.
